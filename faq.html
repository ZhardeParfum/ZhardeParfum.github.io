FAQ

Quick answers about our fragrances and ordering.

What is Extrait de Parfum?

Extrait de parfum is the highest concentration of fragrance oil, which gives a richer scent with longer wear than eau de parfum or eau de toilette. Every ZHARDÉ fragrance is an extrait.

How long does the fragrance last?

Longevity depends on the fragrance, your skin and the weather. As an extrait, ZHARDÉ is made to last through the day. Spray on pulse points on moisturised skin for the best result.

How do I place an order?

Add your perfume, size and units to the cart, then tap Check out. Enter your name, phone number and delivery address, and your order summary opens in WhatsApp, ready to send to us.

What is the Discovery Set?

A set of all nine ZHARDÉ scents in 10ml, so you can try them before choosing a full bottle. Add it to your cart from the homepage.

How does Buy 2, Get Special Price work?

Add 2 or more bottles of 30ml or 100ml and the special price applies automatically: 2 × 30ml RM65, 2 × 100ml RM185, 30ml + 100ml RM125, 3 × 30ml RM95.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we will confirm the delivery method, cost and estimated time.

Can I return my order?

Please WhatsApp us with your order details as soon as you receive it, and we will advise on returns and refunds.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped we will send you the tracking number on WhatsApp.

How do I submit a review?

Open the Reviews page and tap Write a Review. If you have a purchase code from us, enter it to receive the Verified Purchase label.
