FAQ

Quick answers about our fragrances and ordering.

Product

What is the difference between 10ml, 30ml and 100ml?

10ml lets you try a scent, 30ml suits regular use, and 100ml is the full-size bottle. Prices are shown on the Shop page.

How do I choose a fragrance?

Take the Fragrance Finder quiz, or try the Discovery Set: all nine scents in 10ml.

What is Extrait de Parfum?

Extrait de parfum is the highest concentration of fragrance oil. Every ZHARDÉ fragrance is an extrait.

Using your fragrance

How many sprays should I use?

Start with 2 to 3 sprays on pulse points such as the wrists and neck, then add more if you like. Performance such as longevity and projection varies with skin, weather and how you wear it.

How should I store my perfume?

Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat, with the cap on.

Ordering

How do I place an order?

Add your fragrance, size and quantity to the cart (or tap BUY), then choose Cash on Delivery (COD) for Shah Alam and nearby areas, or Door Delivery by post. COD goes straight to WhatsApp, where we arrange the place, time and payment with you. Door Delivery asks for your details first, then payment by DuitNow QR.

What is the Discovery Set?

A set of all nine ZHARDÉ scents in 10ml so you can try them before choosing a full bottle.

How do the Buy 2 and Buy 3 special prices work?

These combinations get a special price automatically in your cart: 3 × 30ml RM95, 2 × 100ml RM190, 3 × 100ml RM275, and 30ml + 100ml RM125. Other combinations, including 2 × 30ml (RM70), are charged at the regular price.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Please WhatsApp us as soon as possible with your Order No and we will advise whether the order can still be changed or cancelled.

Payment and delivery

What payment methods do you accept?

For Cash on Delivery (COD), in Shah Alam and nearby areas only, you can pay when you receive your order or in advance. We will arrange it with you on WhatsApp. For Door Delivery, you pay by DuitNow QR at checkout, then send your order on WhatsApp with your payment screenshot.

Where is COD available?

COD is available in Shah Alam and nearby areas only. For other areas, please choose Door Delivery.

How long does delivery take?

We will confirm the delivery method, cost and estimated time on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped we will send you the tracking number on WhatsApp.

Returns

What is your return and refund policy?

Please WhatsApp us with your Order No as soon as you receive your order, and we will advise on returns and refunds.
