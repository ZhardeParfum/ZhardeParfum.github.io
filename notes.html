Fragrance Notes

Every perfume unfolds in stages. Knowing the notes helps you choose a scent you will love.

Top notes

The first impression. Light and bright, they greet you the moment you spray and are the first to fade.

Heart notes

The character. They emerge as the top notes settle and form the core of the fragrance.

Base notes

The lasting memory. Deep and rich, they linger the longest and give the scent its depth.
